Power BI Sales Dashboard

📊 Project Overview 
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance and provide useful business insights. The dashboard allows users to explore revenue, products, units sold, cities, and sales performance over time.

🎯 Project Objectives:
* Analyze overall sales performance
* Analyze revenue by product
* Analyze units sold by product
* Compare sales performance between cities
* Analyze sales trends over time
* Identify top-performing products and cities
* Provide interactive business insights

📑 Dashboard Pages 
Page 1 — Sales Overview The first page provides an overview of the company's sales performance, including:
* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Units Sold
* Sales Average
* Units Sold by Products
* Revenue by City
* Revenue by Category
* Revenue by Product
* Revenue by Year and Month
* Sales Overview Table

Page 2 — Product Analysis The second page focuses on product performance, including:
* Total Units Sold
* Total Revenue
* Average Revenue per Unit
* Top Product
* Revenue by Product
* Product Sales Ranking
* Average Units Per Order By Product
* Revenue by City & Product
* Product Performance Table
* Revenue Percentage by Product

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* Power BI Desktop
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Visualization
